Permafrost & Potions

A Witch's Guide to Success

Created by: Isabel, Jaylyn, and Vedanti

Time to Explore!

Once upon a time, in a kingdom far, far away, lived an ice witch in a tower. She decided to venture into the enchanted forest to gather more ingredients for her potions.

The Frozen Forest

She realized that the forest had been iced over by the global freezing. She decided to collect frozen organisms to use in her potions. Once she got home, she covered them with a special type of frost so that they would become enchanted.

Back to the Laboratory

Once she finished collecting the ingredients, she placed them in her laboratory to begin brewing her potions. But what kind of magic would she create?

Your Journey Begins

Collect the ingredients and help the ice witch restore her magical world!
